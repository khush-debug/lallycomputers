Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Supreme Infosys (Lally Computers), Sodhi Nagar, Moga, Punjab

1. Overview & Business Model

Supreme Infosys (operating commercially as Lally Computers) operates an in-store electronics showroom, certified pre-owned laptop laboratory, and chip-level repair centre at 5-A, Sodhi Nagar, Moga, Punjab 142001.

This website serves as an informational digital catalogue and in-store stock availability viewer. No online transactions, payments, or automated checkouts take place on this website. Inquiries, specifications verification, and reservations are handled directly via WhatsApp or in person at our store.

2. Information We Collect

  • Local Browser Storage: We store your preference for light/dark theme (`lally_apple_theme`), selected department space, temporary shopping inquiry cart, and optional custom Google Sheets inventory URL locally on your device using `localStorage`. This data never leaves your device unless you voluntarily sync a sheet.
  • WhatsApp Communications: When you click "Inquire on WhatsApp" or "Open Stock", you are redirected to WhatsApp Messenger to communicate with our official phone number (+91 98720 10193). Any contact details or messages shared via WhatsApp are governed by WhatsApp's Privacy Policy and our standard in-store customer support terms.
  • No User Tracking: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not use third-party behavioral advertising trackers, invasive pixels, or data brokers.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

Essential Technical Cookies Only: This website utilizes strictly necessary client-side storage to remember your visual preferences (dark mode), stock filters, and customer cart selections.

You can clear your local storage at any time via your browser's developer tools or settings, or via the "Cookie Preferences" link in the website footer.

4. Live Google Sheets Stock Sync

Our website features an optional Google Sheets inventory sync feature. If you connect a custom public Google Sheet URL, your browser retrieves the spreadsheet text directly from Google's servers. We do not store or mirror your private Google account credentials on any intermediate server.

5. Customer Reviews & Testimonials

Customer reviews featured on this website are publicly submitted by verified customers on Google Maps (Supreme Infosys - Lally Computers Moga). Names and testimonials displayed reflect public feedback left by our patrons.
